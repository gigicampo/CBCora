CBCora is an educational companion

CBCora helps organize CBC values, compare them with the reference ranges printed on your report, and track changes over time. It does not diagnose medical conditions or replace a healthcare professional.

If you feel severely unwell or have urgent symptoms, seek medical care rather than relying on an app result.

User Guide

Getting started with CBCora

A quick guide to scanning, understanding, and tracking your CBC results.

1

Set up your profile

Add your name, basic personal information, and optional photo from the gear button on Home.

2

Scan or upload a CBC

Use Scan a CBC Result to take a photo, choose an image, upload a PDF, or enter values manually.

3

Review extracted values

Check every result, unit, and reference range before saving. Low-confidence OCR readings are highlighted for manual review.

4

Understand your results

Open a CBC measure to see its plain-language meaning, your lab range, status, and result-linked guidance.

5

Use Helpful Tips safely

Review food, activity, hydration, and safety tips as general education. CBCora does not diagnose conditions or replace professional medical advice.

6

Track, compare, and export

Use Trends for detailed charts, compare two saved CBC reports, and use Print / Save as PDF when you want a clean summary to keep or discuss with your clinician.

Important: Seek medical care for urgent symptoms or when advised by a healthcare professional. Do not use CBCora to self-diagnose or start treatment on your own.
